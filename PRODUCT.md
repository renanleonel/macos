# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

People evaluating Renan's work, collaborators, and curious visitors. They should be able to explore the portfolio naturally without learning a custom navigation system.

## Product Purpose

A personal website presented as a faithful, interactive macOS 27 desktop. The operating system is both the navigation model and the memorable proof of front-end craft.

## Positioning

The portfolio does not merely describe thoughtful interface engineering; it demonstrates it through a complete desktop experience.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: explore the work through Finder and Safari.
- Secondary CTA: start a conversation through Messages or Mail.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: a personal portfolio that feels like using a real Mac.
- Belief ladder: this is delightful; it is carefully built; the work behind it is worth exploring; the person behind it is worth contacting.
- Proof on hand: placeholder project files and content until real work is supplied.

## Brand Personality

Precise, playful, and quietly technical. The emotional goal is immediate delight followed by confidence in the implementation detail.

## Anti-references

Avoid a conventional portfolio landing page, generic card grids, fake terminal-only aesthetics, and superficial glassmorphism without real window behavior.

## Design Principles

- Make the operating system useful, not decorative.
- Reward curiosity with working interactions.
- Match macOS spatial behavior before adding portfolio flourishes.
- Keep the content replaceable without flattening the experience.
- Preserve legibility over complex wallpaper and translucent materials.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, strong focus states, reduced motion, semantic controls, and a usable compact layout on smaller screens.
